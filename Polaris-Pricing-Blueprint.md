# Polaris Pricing Blueprint

## Positioning

Polaris builds fast, modern business websites using React, TypeScript,
and Vercel. Optional backend functionality is powered by Supabase.

The goal is to sell complete business solutions, not just websites.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Packages

## Starter --- £795

Ideal for: - Tradespeople - Restaurants - Barbers - Cafes - Local
businesses

Includes: - Up to 5 pages - Responsive design - Contact form - Basic
SEO - Google Maps integration - Domain & DNS setup - Vercel deployment -
2 rounds of revisions - 30 days of post-launch support

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Business --- £1,195

Everything in Starter, plus: - Up to 10 pages - Enhanced animations -
Image optimisation - Analytics setup - Improved SEO - Static blog
support

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Custom Web App --- From £2,000

For projects requiring backend functionality.

Examples: - Supabase database - Authentication - Booking systems - Admin
dashboards - Customer portals - API integrations - Bespoke software

Do not charge "for a database." Charge for the business feature it
enables.

Example add-ons: - Contact submissions database: £200 - Admin dashboard:
£500+ - User accounts: £600+ - Booking system: £700--£1,200+ - Customer
portal: £1,000+

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Monthly Care Plans

## No Care Plan (£0/month)

Client owns the project.

Changes are billed at: - £50/hour

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Care Plan --- £39/month

Includes: - Hosting management - SSL monitoring - Domain management -
Uptime monitoring - Minor bug fixes - One small content update (up to 15
minutes)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Growth Plan --- £99/month

Everything in Care Plan plus: - Up to 1 hour of content edits each
month - Performance monitoring - Priority support - Monthly backups

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Sales Philosophy

Sell outcomes instead of technologies.

Avoid: - "Includes Supabase" - "Built with React"

Instead sell: - Online bookings - Customer enquiries - Professional
online presence - Faster websites - Mobile-friendly experience - Easy
updates - Better Google visibility

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Pricing Strategy

Use fixed-price projects instead of hourly estimates whenever possible.

Businesses prefer certainty.

Current pricing: - Starter: £795 - Business: £1,195 - Custom Web Apps:
From £2,000 - Care Plan: £39/month - Growth Plan: £99/month

Increase prices as the portfolio grows.
